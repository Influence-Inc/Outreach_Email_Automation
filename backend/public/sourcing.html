Scout Instagram creators

Automatically discover creators on the Instagram app (via a paired phone) who post about your keywords and go viral often. Shortlisted creators go to the Find creators page (Curated by INFLUENCE Team).

What to look for

Each keyword is searched on Instagram. Jev also reads from them the topic, niche, genre and reel format to look for — e.g. “gym workout tutorial” → Fitness, Tutorial.

A reel counts as viral at this many views (100K if left empty). We read each creator’s newest 16 reels and keep the ones whose reels go viral often: about a third of them, and at least 2 — e.g. 5 of 16, 4 of 12, 2 of 5. A creator one viral reel short goes to review.

The run keeps going until this many creators are shortlisted (or the keywords run out of results).

The brand

How to scout

Each phone decides how to scout: a new account warms up first — it searches your keywords and likes the reel of every creator it shortlists — then scouts its own reel feed, and goes back to warming up whenever the feed stops serving the right creators.
